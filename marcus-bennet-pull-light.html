CASE STUDY — FAISS

Domain: E-Commerce Recommendation System Using FAISS

CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

What Is My Case Study?

This case study explores FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search), a high-performance library developed by Meta AI Research for efficient similarity search and clustering of dense vectors.

The study examines how FAISS can power E-Commerce Recommendation Systems by identifying products with similar feature representations using vector embeddings and similarity-search techniques.

As part of the practical implementation, I developed an AI-Based Product Recommendation System using a synthetic dataset of 3,000 products. The system generates 64-dimensional embeddings, builds a FAISS index, performs similarity searches, and recommends the Top 5 most similar products based on a customer's selected product.

KEY FOCUS AREAS
  • FAISS architecture and similarity search.
  • Vector embeddings and indexing techniques.
  • IndexFlatL2 and advanced FAISS indexes.
  • AI-based E-Commerce Product Recommendations.
  • Retrieval of Top 5 similar products.
